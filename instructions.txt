--------------------------------------------------
LESSONS & BEST PRACTICES LEARNED IN PHASE 1
--------------------------------------------------

1. Opacity Modifiers (e.g. zinc-900/90):
   - In Tailwind, the slash `/` controls opacity.
   - `zinc-900/90` means the color `zinc-900` at 90% opacity.
   - Great for hover states and subtle overlays without picking mismatched colors.

2. Protecting Buttons from Mobile Squishing:
   - `whitespace-nowrap` inside Button baseStyles prevents button labels from awkwardly breaking into multiple lines ("Add" / "to" / "Cart") when space is tight.
   - `shrink-0` on icon wrappers prevents SVGs from distorting or flattening.
   - Always remember that flex containers default to `flex-row nowrap`. For rows of buttons or chips, add `flex-wrap` so items wrap onto new lines on smaller screens.

     * outline: border border-zinc-200 text-zinc-800 dark:border-zinc-700 dark:text-zinc-300
